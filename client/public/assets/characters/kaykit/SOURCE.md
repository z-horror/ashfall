Included files:
- `character.glb`: Rigged humanoid character mesh (based on Rogue model, Rig_Medium)
- `animations_general.glb`: General humanoid animations (Idle_A, Hit_A, Death_A, etc.)
- `animations_movement.glb`: Basic movement humanoid animations (Walking_A, Running_A, etc.)
- `animations_combat.glb`: Melee combat humanoid animations (Melee_1H_Attack_Slice_Horizontal, etc.)
